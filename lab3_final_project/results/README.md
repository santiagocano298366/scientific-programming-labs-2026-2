# results

Everything here is saved by `python main.py`.

* `tables/`: summary of the features by group (mean, median and variance), outliers, group tests, separability (Fisher ratio and AUC), confusion matrix, metrics by class, the 30 repeated splits and the eigenvalues of the covariance.
* `figures/`: curves by group, densities of the features, confusion matrix and ROC curves, comparison of the sets of features, fit and derivative of one curve and covariance.

The confusion matrix has the true classes in the rows and the predicted classes in the columns.
